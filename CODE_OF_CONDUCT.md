# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference, and applies it to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where the project is represented.

In short: be respectful and constructive; accept criticism of work gracefully; focus on what is best for the project; do not harass, insult, or publish other people's private information. Participants who behave unacceptably may have contributions edited, rejected or removed, and may be blocked from the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the contact in `Contact.md`. Reports are handled in confidence, and the person reporting will not face retaliation.
